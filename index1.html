Notation
How to hold the cube & read the moves

Hold it exactly like the front view: the top colour on top, the front colour facing you. Never rotate the whole cube while following the steps.

R U FTurn the Right / Up / Front outer face a quarter turn clockwise (as if you were looking straight at that face).
L D BLeft / Down / Back outer face, clockwise looking at that face.
'   2R' = counter-clockwise, R2 = half turn.
2R, 3U …Turn only that one inner layer: 2R is the 2nd layer in from the right face, turned the same way as R.
2-3RTurn layers 2 and 3 (counted in from the right face) together, leaving the outer R face still.
Rw, 3RwWCA wide turns: the outer 2 (or 3) layers together. In WCA mode a single inner layer is written as two moves, e.g. 2R = Rw R'.
M E S3×3 only: the middle layer (M follows L, E follows D, S follows F).

The solver builds the centres first, then pairs up the edge pieces (turn an inner layer, line pieces up, turn it back), then solves the rest like a 3×3, and finally fixes any leftover edge pieces. In the final edge stage each step only moves the few pieces marked with pink dots, so finished pieces stay finished.
